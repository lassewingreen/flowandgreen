QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

GOOD TO KNOW.

Can you play our special song?

We’re happy to learn one or more songs if we agree on it as part of the booking and have enough notice. We can also discuss the tempo and length of your first dance.

How long do you play for?

We agree on this around your plans. We play for the agreed period with short breaks, which can often fit around speeches and other contributions.

Do you bring sound equipment?

Yes, we bring the sound equipment needed for most weddings and parties. For very large venues or outdoor events, we discuss the requirements with you beforehand.

Can you play outdoors?

Yes, if the weather and conditions allow it and there’s a power supply nearby. We agree on a backup plan in case of bad weather.

Do you play at other events?

Yes, we also play at birthdays, company parties, summer parties and other events.
