THE PRACTICAL DETAILS

ONE CLEAR
QUOTE.

Where do we play?

We are based in Aarhus and play events in Jutland, Funen, Zealand, Lolland and Falster by arrangement. Send your date and location so we can confirm the options.

What does it cost?

The price depends on your date, location, playing time and which parts of your event need music. We include travel in one quote, so you know the cost before deciding.

Sound and arrangements

We bring our sound equipment and agree on the schedule, volume and song requests beforehand. We discuss outdoor performances or large venues with you in advance.

QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS

GOOD TO KNOW.

Can we book music for just part of the day?

Yes. Ask us about your ceremony, reception, dinner or dancing. We can also combine several parts of the day.

Can you play our special song?

We welcome requests. We agree any special ceremony or first-dance song in advance so we have time to prepare it.

What about sound equipment and breaks?

We bring the sound equipment needed for a typical wedding and agree breaks with you. For large venues or outdoor events, we discuss the requirements together.

What does it cost?

It depends on the event. We provide a no-obligation quote based on playing time, location and your wishes, so the arrangements are clear before you book.

Does an enquiry reserve our date?

An enquiry does not reserve the date. We usually reply within 24 hours and confirm the booking and details once we have reached an agreement.
