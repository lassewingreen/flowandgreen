# Flow & Green: content evidence and launch checks

Checked 9 November 2026. Public site copy is based on the existing Flow & Green HTML. The JSON contains restrained Danish and English campaign copy, a privacy draft and three sourced review excerpts.

## Reviews and coverage

[Eventzonen: Flow & Green](https://eventzonen.dk/flowandgreen) was accessible. Three excerpts in the JSON were checked against the named reviews and their dates: Trine Schmidt Berggreen (15 September 2026), Ane Lorentzen (15 November 2024, company Christmas party), and Søren Rasmussen (28 October 2024, wedding reception). The Danish excerpts total 12 words; their English translations total 11 words. Mark the English versions as translated from Danish. Do not imply a quotation was originally written in English.

The profile identifies Aarhus as the base and lists Jutland, Funen, Zealand, Lolland and Falster. The JSON's coverage copy follows that list, rather than asserting every location in Denmark. The profile also publishes a DKK 6,000–17,000 range; keep that out of campaign copy until the owner confirms it remains current and explains what it includes. Do not replace the website's own reply-time promise with the platform's response metric.

## Privacy sources

- [Formspree security](https://formspree.io/security/): Formspree states its services are hosted with AWS in the United States and says it relies on Standard Contractual Clauses as a processor. Do not claim EU-only hosting.
- [Formspree privacy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/): describes submission delivery, collection of technical information and international processing.
- [Formspree current plans](https://formspree.io/plans/): the public free tier is shown with 50 monthly submissions and a 30-day submission archive. Verify the actual account's plan/usage; an inbox copy can outlive the dashboard archive. Do not promise automatic deletion of all enquiry copies after 30 days.
- [Google Analytics safeguarding data](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245?hl=en): measurement uses technical identifiers; customers must not send personally identifying information to Analytics.
- [Google privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=da): provider policy covering Google services including YouTube and email.
- [YouTube embedding](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780?hl=en): Privacy Enhanced Mode uses youtube-nocookie.com. It does not justify describing video playback as free of data processing.
- [Meta privacy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/): direct policy URL supplied for user access. Automated retrieval redirected to a login/temporary-block page, so no detailed claims about current Meta transfer arrangements are made in the draft.
- [GitHub Pages data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages): GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.
- [GitHub privacy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement): hosting-provider privacy information.
- [Datatilsynet: cookies and GDPR for small businesses](https://www.datatilsynet.dk/regler-og-vejledning/gdpr-univers-for-smaa-virksomheder/cookies-og-gdpr): explains consent and user information about optional technologies.
- [Datatilsynet: individual rights](https://www.datatilsynet.dk/borger/hvad-er-dine-rettigheder): access, correction and other rights.

## Owner and account checks before ad launch

1. Confirm the public coverage and any price example. The live Eventzonen profile is evidence, but owner instructions take priority.
2. Confirm who is the legal data controller behind the brand, including any business name/address/CVR details that should be added to the contact block. The draft uses the known brand and contact email without inventing legal identity.
3. Review the actual retention of enquiries in the Formspree dashboard, Gmail inbox, exports and any booking records. The draft describes purposes and retention criteria, not an unimplemented automatic deletion schedule.
4. Review the account's processor terms/transfer safeguards for Formspree and the mail provider. No new provider agreement has been accepted by this work.
5. Reconcile privacy wording with the final consent code: category names, 180-day local choice, Analytics cookie duration and session attribution. Update wording if code changes.
6. When the owner supplies measurement IDs, check provider/account data retention, consent settings, automatic/enhanced form measurement and linked ad features. Keep form contents out of all measurement events.
7. Verify the receiving email address, notification delivery, spam handling, form quota and a monitored follow-up routine in the actual Formspree account. These cannot be established by editing static files.
8. Recheck real browser network requests/cookies before choice, after rejection, after category-specific acceptance and after withdrawal. Test both languages and mobile layouts.
